## Descricao curta

Industrial, high-contrast Plasma 6 theme system for the RAGOS desktop, with a technical dark flagship and a restrained corporate light variant.

## Descricao longa

RAGOS Control is the Plasma 6 desktop language used by the RAGOS client runtime. It combines a precise technical accent blue, restrained industrial surfaces, high legibility and coherent wallpapers for dark and light deployments. The package includes Global Themes, Plasma Styles, Color Schemes and wallpapers designed to match the visual direction already used by the RAGOS boot and login surfaces.
